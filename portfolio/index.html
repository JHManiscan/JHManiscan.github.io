Thesis

Self-Inflating Life Vest Integrated with Pulse Rate Technology and Notification

The Self-Inflating Life Vest is designed to provide personal safety by automatically providing flotation support and alerting lifeguards during emergencies. The system integrates pulse rate monitoring technology using a MAX30102 pulse rate detector to detect abnormal physiological signals—such as a spike in pulse—that may indicate distress. When such a condition is identified, the vest automatically inflates to ensure immediate flotation and simultaneously sends a real-time notification to the designated lifeguard.
